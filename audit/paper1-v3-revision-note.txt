BITCOIN EXIT DOMINANCE — REVISION NOTE, NOVEMBER 2026 (v3)

Readers identified that the coalition proof must evaluate payoffs after a
proposed coordinated move. This revision includes each allocator's capital
weight, states the coalitional-persistence condition (NR) explicitly, and
proves that the greatest high-Exit Nash equilibrium, when one exists, is
strong Nash and hence BPW coalition-proof. The threshold ratchet is retained;
the inference that every adoption plateau must be temporary is removed.
Assumptions A1–A4, Properties P1–P7, and the economic argument are retained.
Theorem summaries are aligned with the repaired proof.

NR is additional formal payoff content: every returning group contains a
member who does not gain at the resulting adoption level. The derivative
conditions do not establish NR. The new proof shows precisely how NR and the
greatest-equilibrium construction yield the BPW result.

The title remains Bitcoin Exit Dominance in Monetary Coordination Games.
The original A4, related work, acknowledgements, author declarations and
bibliographic entries are retained. The May revision note remains as history.
No new game-theory citation is needed: BPW (1987) and Milgrom–Shannon (1994)
were already cited. Exact source passages and deductions accompany the redline.
