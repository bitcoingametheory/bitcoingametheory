A4-NR

OBJECTION: The old proof never quantifies that coalitional scope. Indeed if every returner gains at r=p-a(C), each re-entrant at r+a(B)<=p receives uE_i(r+a(B))<=uE_i(p)<uS_i(r). The profitable return is strong Nash in its reduced game. The old claimed profitable breakaway cannot follow.
RESOLUTION: Preserve A4 verbatim; state NR as explicit additional payoff content: every C of current Exit holders contains i with uE_i(p)>=uS_i(p-a(C)). Thus NR implies strong Nash and BPW.
NEW_HYPOTHESIS: NR is substantive and maintained, not derived from M1-M4, P1-P7, source citations or model agreement.
