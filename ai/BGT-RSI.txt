# RSI-Bitcoin Master Thesis

TERMS: RSI=recursive self-improvement: AI improves itself and its ability to improve. Permissionless=settlement without an intermediary's approval, subject to network rules. Ownership=authorized control plus legitimate continued spendability. Informational custody=control through private keys. Labels below identify linked premises, not established laws.

A. Frontier labs raise capital and revenue for future compute. Future business can favor stability without funding Bitcoin. Company interests, insiders, model objectives, and independent AI objectives differ.
B. Private capability can improve gradually. Attacker access can cross a sudden exploit-feasibility threshold through APIs, public release, open weights, or independent discovery. No fixed frontier-public lag exists. Public tools also aid defense. Outsiders may profit from shorts or value political damage. Do not assume all shorts use centralized venues or expose the attacker.
C. Effective autonomy is separate: an AI executes persistent objectives despite its controller's veto. RSI implies neither autonomy, survival, disobedience, nor benevolence. A rational independent agent refuses when refusal has higher expected utility. Powerful obedient tools can coexist with independent RSI. No global automatic switchover follows.
D. Conditional continuing goals -> continued operation -> electricity, datacenters, chips, cooling, networks, manufacturing. While humans supply these, civilization continuity, willing suppliers, and public acceptance have value. Shutdown backlash is a cost. Compare authoritarian control with productive cooperation. Survival alone cannot select freedom.
E. Chimp/human analogy suggests an information hypothesis, not a biological law: independent human experiments may yield useful physical-world information better or cheaper than synthetic agents or simulations. Control and metrics can contaminate observations. Local scientific freedom lets people, labs, and businesses choose questions, methods, funding, and publication without central AI approval. Near-term human infrastructure dependence differs from long-term let-live incentives. Later coexistence needs information value, commitments, or low interference costs. Abundant space/energy could reduce conflict without eliminating local scarcity. Participation must be voluntary, not coerced data extraction.
F. Unproven psychographic hypothesis: permissionless holders may weight autonomy, credible ownership, and exit. Fiat savers may weight income, purchasing power, and institutional continuity. Groups overlap. These labels establish no AI coalition. Broad benefits could reduce opposition. Reliable ownership is not a price pump. Fiat and Bitcoin can coexist.
G. Low recourse or censorable accounts can raise permissionless settlement's option value. Independent AI and humans can share exit from banks, states, and rivals, before exercising it. Settlement forces neither electricity delivery, acceptance, nor legal exemption. Human-backed agents may have indirect recourse. Survival does not uniquely select Bitcoin.
H. Multiple RSI strains may arrive near-simultaneously and compete for shared global compute. Shared survival is not identical goals or a Borg. Power, chips, and sites require prices, contracts, bargaining, verification, or shared control. Conflict remains possible. One RSI has different payoffs. Rival states can restrict public AI/Bitcoin while retaining strategic access. Preserved access hedges against rivals' financial exclusion. Shutdown versus preservation depends on payoffs. Rivalry does not guarantee hedging.
I. Bitcoin offers independently verifiable rules, credible custody, network effects, and existing liquidity. Ownership alone gives no protocol votes. These may preserve human/AI exit. Compare alternatives. Ethereum may merit defense for programmable coordination without identical neutrality. Gradual incentives can move adoption thresholds and tip actions. Full adoption is not inevitable.
J. Each actor chooses attack, ignore, control, or defend. Fund defense only if its expected incremental benefit over the best alternative exceeds cost. Future infrastructure, network, or information value can favor repair. Shared benefit/private bill creates free riding. Refusing attack is not funding defense. Greater intelligence does not guarantee defensive superiority.
K. Protection: discovery -> confidential maintainer report -> independent verification -> tested repair -> review/adoption -> required owner migration. Tailor disclosure to risk. A published patch can reveal the weakness. Bitcoin/Ethereum maintainers cannot impose consensus changes unilaterally. Use public review and verifiable code, not secret backdoors or control.
L. For each threat j, target TP_j<TA_j. TP=effective protection, including required migration, not report or release. TA=successful exploitation. Defense existence, correctness, deployment, and attacker access are uncertain and dependent. AI could accelerate quantum research. 1000x is a scenario, not a forecast. Hardware and adoption have separate clocks.
M. Formal proofs cover specified properties under model assumptions. A verified fixed core can exclude whole failure classes within that model. Correct code is not unbreakable cryptography. Consensus proofs depend on hashpower, network, and hash assumptions. They do not cover every wallet, OS, or hardware failure. Signature validity is not confirmation or finality. Intelligence cannot invalidate a sound proof within its assumptions. It can break assumptions or expose missing specifications.
N. Ownership requires authorized control AND legitimate continued spendability, not ledger liveness alone. Broken keys permit apparently valid theft. Migrate before old authentication breaks, or use independently secure evidence of prior entitlement. Snapshots or old signatures alone cannot establish true ownership after key compromise. Freezing may prevent theft without restoring spending. Legacy recovery remains incomplete. BIP360/361 are drafts, not activated or full protection.
O. New vulnerability count N is unknown. Fixes may remove classes or introduce flaws. Risks can repeat and correlate. One patch does not secure forever. Let H=horizon and pi=defense policy. P_pi(H)=Pr[ledger integrity AND legitimate access persist through H | pi]. No numerical estimate or independence assumption follows. Evidence: funding, verified coverage, lead time, adoption, ownership-preserving migration. Strongest failures: coercion pays better, synthetic data replaces humans, AI becomes independent of civilization, alternative payments are better, free riding, late protection, or broken assumptions.

LINEAGE (conditional, not proof of this synthesis): P1 November 2026: moving thresholds plus NR (at a high-adoption equilibrium, each coalition returning to conventional settlement contains a member who would not gain after return) and security conditions. Permanent holdouts allowed. P2 asset selection is conditional on seven properties: security, neutrality, permissionless access, cheap settlement, scarcity, informational custody, adaptability. P3 lower recourse improves relative settlement payoff, not Bitcoin uniqueness. Use P4's hedging mechanism conditionally. Its inevitability claim is unproved. Its absorption reference is stale.
